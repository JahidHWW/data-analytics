## Projects

### 1. Retail Sales Performance Analysis

**Technologies:** PostgreSQL, SQL, Tableau, Docker

Analyzed 62,884 retail sales line items to explore product performance, revenue trends, average order value, and data quality.

**Key Findings:**
- Approximately $54.72M in estimated revenue (2016–2020).
- Computers generated the highest revenue among product categories.
- Identified recurring 35–37-day gaps in recorded transactions.
